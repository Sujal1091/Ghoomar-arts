Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Ghoomar Arts by S R Creations

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Ghoomar Arts by S R Creations (“we”, “us”), Zaveri Bazar, Mumbai, is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. We handle it in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law.

What we collect

  • Contact details — your name, phone / WhatsApp number and email when you contact us or place an order.
  • Order details — the products you ask about or buy, your delivery address, and invoice details such as a GSTIN if you give us one for business purchases.
  • Payment references — UPI or bank transaction IDs to match your payment. We do not collect or store card numbers, PINs or passwords.
  • Messages — what you send us on WhatsApp, phone or email.

This website has no account sign-up and no contact form. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To answer enquiries and send prices, photos and availability.
  • To process, invoice, pack and deliver your order, and to handle returns and exchanges.
  • To keep accounts and tax records as required by law.
  • To send you news of new collections, only if you have agreed to receive it. You can say “stop” at any time.

Who we share it with

  • Courier and logistics partners, to deliver your order.
  • Banks and payment apps, to receive and refund payments.
  • Our accountants and government authorities, where the law requires it (for example GST filings).

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

When you message us on WhatsApp, your messages are also handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep order and invoice records for as long as tax and accounting laws require (generally up to 8 years). Enquiries that do not lead to an order are deleted when no longer needed, normally within 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent to marketing messages. Some records must be kept by law even after a deletion request. Write to us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

We keep customer records on secured devices with limited access, and share only what partners need to do their job. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your data.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version and its effective date are always on this page.
