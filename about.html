Our story

Zaveri Bazar has been Mumbai's jewellery market for generations. From a ground-floor shop in Fulker Niwas, we bring together traditional Indian craft — kundan, meenakari, temple work, polki-style and oxidised finishes — in designs that are made to be worn, not kept in a locker.

We named ourselves after the Ghoomar, Rajasthan's twirling folk dance. The swirl of the ghagra, the jingle of the payal and the shine of the borla are what we try to capture in every piece.

What we do

Retail — walk in, try on, and take home pieces for your wedding, sangeet, Navratri or everyday look.

Bulk & reseller orders — boutiques, stylists and online sellers can ask for wholesale quantities and repeat designs.
