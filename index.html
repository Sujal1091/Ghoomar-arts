why Ghoomar

Rooted in Rajasthan, made for today

Ghoomar is the swirling folk dance of Rajasthan — colour, movement and celebration. Our jewellery carries the same spirit: rich traditional craft in pieces that are comfortable to wear and easy on the pocket.
